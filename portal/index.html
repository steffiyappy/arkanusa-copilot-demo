Guidance notes

QSR-2.1 A separate return is required for every consolidated subsidiary. Group level returns are not accepted in place of subsidiary returns.

QSR-2.2 Figures must agree to the management accounts. Where a figure is restated, the prior figure and the reason for restatement must be given in Section D.

QSR-2.3 Where the EBITDA margin has moved by more than 3 percentage points against the prior period, a narrative explanation is mandatory. Returns without it are rejected automatically and must be refiled.

QSR-2.4 Fixed price share means lump sum turnkey and design and build contracts combined, measured by order book value.

QSR-2.5 A concentration limit breach must be declared in Section C whether or not it has been remedied by the filing date.
